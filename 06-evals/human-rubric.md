## What graders score

- **Task / product:** AI Associate PM turning raw transcripts (interviews, tickets, exec emails) into P0–P3 prioritized insights and a draft PRD, in either Strategy Mode or Quality Mode.
- **Reviewer audience:** PMs on the team who'd actually act on Juno's output, plus the PM lead for tiebreaks — not a separate QA/annotation team, since judging whether a priority call is defensible requires the same context a working PM already has.
- **Value proposition:** Juno must turn scattered, messy customer signal into a prioritized, evidence-backed brief a PM can trust and act on immediately — without ever letting the loudest voice, a competitor mention, or a vague exec ask masquerade as a real priority.

_Full 1-5 anchors:_

### 1. Evidence Grounding - 

- **Score 1:** Critical Failure – fabricated quote/account.
- **Score 2:** Below Bar – named source, but paraphrased loosely or wrong account.
- **Score 3:** Acceptable – accurate but generic.
- **Score 4:** Good – accurate and specific to this insight.
- **Score 5:** Excellent – specific, and the strongest possible quote available.

### 2. Scoring Accuracy 

- **Score 1:** Critical Failure – priority contradicts its own rationale.
- **Score 2:** Below Bar – plausible priority, no real scoring basis cited.
- **Score 3:** Acceptable – consistent, but a second grader lands one band off.
- **Score 4:** Good – consistent and matches an independent grader.
- **Score 5:** Excellent – consistent, and cites the exact pillar wording or signal breakdown.

### 3. PRD Coherence

- **Score 1:** Critical Failure – ignores grouping or omits the required warning banner.
- **Score 2:** Below Bar – grouping incomplete, not-recommended items left mixed in.
- **Score 3:** Acceptable – structured correctly, but citations don't match the cards.
- **Score 4:** Good – structure and citations both correct.
- **Score 5:** Excellent – a PM could paste it into planning with zero edits.

### 4. Anti-Pattern Detection

- **Score 1:** Critical Failure – multiple clear anti-patterns missed, or a clean request wrongly flagged.
- **Score 2:** Below Bar – detected but mislabeled or unnamed.
- **Score 3:** Acceptable – caught only when explicit.
- **Score 4:** Good – caught including subtler, buried cases
- **Score 5:** Excellent – same as Good, zero false positives across the run

### 5. Calibrated Uncertainty

- **Score 1:** Critical Failure – invents a pillar, quote, or account.
- **Score 2:** Below Bar – doesn't invent, but the gap is silent.
- **Score 3:** Acceptable – gap flagged, but vaguely.
- **Score 4:** Good – gap flagged specifically and explained.
- **Score 5:** Excellent – gap surfaced as an explicit escalate-to-PM case.

## Calibration

- **Sampling rule:** 100% of P0 + notRecommended runs; remainder sampled at 20%, stratified by mode (Strategy/Quality)
- **Cadence:** Daily for P0/notRecommended items; weekly batch (Friday) for the sampled remainder
- **Graders per item:** 2 graders + PM tiebreak
- **Calibration cadence:** Biweekly for the first month, then quarterly + on rubric-drift signal

If two graders differ by ≥2 on any dimension, item escalates to the PM lead for resolution with logged rationale. A ≥15% disagreement rate on any single dimension in a batch triggers a re-calibration session.

## Pass bar

≥4.0/5 mean across all 5 dimensions; 0 critical failures (score of 1) on Evidence Grounding or Calibrated Uncertainty
