## Controls

- **Kill switch:** Aborts when: the AI call fails twice (one retry on a transient 429/502, then stop — no infinite retry loop), the hard timeout ceiling (~15s) is hit, the response can't be parsed into valid JSON after that one retry, or the transcript input is empty to begin with. Every abort returns a clear error to the toast — never a silent hang, never a guessed result.
- **Rate / cost caps:** Every tool returns a typed, bounded object, never free text. Reads return fixed records (ticket + account + timestamp, ARR + tier). The AI call is forced through a structured schema — required fields only for id/title/description/priority, everything strategic optional and explicitly nullable, so "no match" is a clear null, never a guess. Failures return a typed error, never a partial or invented result.
- **Escalate-on-stuck:** If the AI call fails, times out, or returns something unparseable, Juno shows nothing rather than a guess — a clear toast explaining what happened, with the failure logged server-side, and the PM can just retry. If a confidence gate triggers instead (low score, multiple anti-patterns, no pillar match), the insight isn't hidden — it's shown flagged as not-recommended with its reason, pulled out of the main priority list, so the PM sees exactly what Juno couldn't resolve on its own rather than a silent gap.

## Monitoring

**Confidence thresholds (map to actions):**

≥70% alignment (Strategy) / ≥80 quality score (Quality): proceeds straight into the PRD at P0/P1 — no extra review gate, though the PM still signs off before it ships.

40–69% / 50–79: included at P2, but visibly marked medium — Juno doesn't block it, but the rationale line is there specifically so the PM double-checks before treating it as committed.

Below 40% / below 50, or 2+ anti-patterns, or no pillar match at all: mandatory human-in-the-loop. Flagged notRecommended, pulled out of the main priority list into its own section, and it takes an explicit PM override to act on it anyway.

**Checkpoints:**

Risk: any insight that would deprioritize something a high-ARR account depends on — the dissent guardrail forces a checkpoint before it can move forward.

Value: anything landing at P0 (Strategy Mode's top band) — the highest-stakes call gets a checkpoint by default, not just the flagged ones.

Ambiguity: 2+ anti-patterns, a quality score under 20, or no strategy-pillar match at all — Juno can't resolve these on its own, so they're pulled out and handed back rather than silently ranked.

**North Star (re-read every loop):**

You surface evidence-weighted priority, you never decide it. In Strategy Mode, the loaded document is the only source of truth — not outside knowledge of the company or the market. When evidence is thin or a match isn't clear, say so with a null or a low score — never invent a pillar, a quote, or a citation to fill the gap. The PM ships the decision; you exist only to stop them from making it on incomplete or biased signal

## Permissions

Juno reads tickets, transcripts, the session-scoped strategy document, and account ARR/tier — nothing more. It writes nothing: no ticket, roadmap item, or record in any connected system gets created, updated, or deleted by Juno itself. The only thing it produces is the insights and PRD draft shown to the PM; turning that into a change to a real system requires the PM's own action, through their own access, not Juno's.
